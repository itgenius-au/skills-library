## Superpowers (obra/superpowers)

Several skills in this repository are adapted from the **Superpowers** Claude Code
plugin by **Jesse Vincent** (https://github.com/obra/superpowers), which is
licensed under the MIT License.

The following skills are derived from Superpowers, in whole or in part:

- `dev-brainstorming` - adapted from `superpowers:brainstorming` (the
  spike/bounded/architectural tiering, the hard approval gate, and the visual
  companion).
- `dev-writing-plans` - adapted from `superpowers:writing-plans` (the task
  template shape, task right-sizing, and the file-structure-first step).
- `dev-qa-verify` - adapted from `superpowers:verification-before-completion`
  (the "passing gates are not proof of done" discipline).
- `dev-debugging` - adapted from `superpowers:systematic-debugging` (the
  reproduce, hypothesize, isolate, root-cause, verify discipline).

Each of these skills also carries an inline credit near the top of its `SKILL.md`.
