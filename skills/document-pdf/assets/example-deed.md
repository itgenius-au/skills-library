# Deed of Release

# Example Company Pty Ltd and Jane Example

This deed is made on the date it is last signed by the parties below. It records an arrangement agreed on or about 1 September 2026 and is not back-dated.

## Parties

1. **Example Company Pty Ltd** ACN 000 000 000 of 1 Example Street, Sydney NSW 2000 (**Company**).
2. **Jane Example** of 2 Sample Road, Exampletown (**Contractor**).

## Operative provisions

**1. Payment.** The Company will pay the Contractor AUD 5,000 within 7 days after this deed is signed by both parties.

**2. Release.** In exchange for the payment in clause 1, the Contractor releases the Company from all claims arising from the engagement, except:

(a) claims that cannot be released by law; and

(b) claims for fraud.

**3. Governing law.** This deed is governed by the laws of New South Wales, Australia.
